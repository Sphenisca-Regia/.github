# Code of conduct

Be useful and be decent.

- Argue about code, not people. Strong opinions are welcome; contempt isn't.
- No harassment, slurs, sexual content, or personal attacks, in any space this
  org runs: issues, PRs, discussions, chat.
- Assume people are here to help until they prove otherwise.

Maintainers will edit or delete comments, lock threads, and ban accounts that
break this. To report something, use **Report content** on the comment
and choose "Report to repository admins".
